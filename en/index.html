Welcome to Königsbrück Heath and Gohrischheide

  • Trails & interactive map
  • Guided tours
  • Rules for visitors

North of the town of Königsbrück in Saxony lies one of the largest nature reserves in the state. For almost a century the area was used for military training. Today the landscape is left to develop freely.

Near Zeithain on the River Elbe, the Gohrischheide and Elbniederterrasse Zeithain nature reserve protects dry heathland and open sandy areas. Both reserves are managed by the same reserve administration.

Discover

Around 6,932 hectares of protected land, recognised as a wilderness area (IUCN category Ib) and part of the Natura 2000 network.

Visit

Large parts of the area must not be entered because of remaining munitions. Please stay on the marked visitor trails at all times.
